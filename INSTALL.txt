ThaiPTT — Push-to-talk dictation (Thai / English) for macOS 13 or later
=======================================================================

INSTALL
1. Drag ThaiPTT.app onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Eject the disk image.

FIRST LAUNCH (the app is not notarized by Apple)
macOS will say "ThaiPTT" cannot be opened / Apple could not verify it.
  - Double-click ThaiPTT in Applications once, click "Done" / "OK".
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click
    "Open Anyway" next to ThaiPTT, then confirm with your password.
Alternative (Terminal):
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ThaiPTT.app
    open /Applications/ThaiPTT.app

PERMISSIONS
ThaiPTT lives in the menu bar (microphone icon). It needs:
  - Microphone and Speech Recognition: click "Allow" when asked.
  - Accessibility: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility,
    turn on ThaiPTT. Then quit ThaiPTT (menu > Quit) and open it again.
The status line in the menu should show: Mic ✓ · Speech ✓ · Accessibility ✓

USE
Hold Right Option (change it in menu > Push-to-Talk Key), speak, release.
The text is pasted at the cursor. A waveform shows at the bottom of the
screen while you speak. Language: menu > Language (Automatic, Thai, English).

If the menu shows "Apple servers" instead of "On-device", add Thai/English in
System Settings > Keyboard > Dictation so macOS downloads the on-device model.
