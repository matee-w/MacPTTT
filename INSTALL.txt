MacPTTT (Mac Push Talk to Text) — push-to-talk dictation (Thai / English) for macOS 13+
==========================================================================

INSTALL
1. Drag MacPTTT.app onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Eject the disk image.

FIRST LAUNCH (the app is not notarized by Apple)
macOS will say "MacPTTT" cannot be opened / Apple could not verify it.
  - Double-click MacPTTT in Applications once, click "Done" / "OK".
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click
    "Open Anyway" next to MacPTTT, then confirm with your password.
Alternative (Terminal):
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacPTTT.app
    open /Applications/MacPTTT.app

PERMISSIONS
MacPTTT lives in the menu bar (speech bubble icon). It needs:
  - Microphone and Speech Recognition: click "Allow" when asked.
  - Accessibility: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility,
    turn on MacPTTT. Then quit MacPTTT (menu > Quit) and open it again.
The status line in the menu should show: Offline · Mic ✓ · Speech ✓ · Accessibility ✓

USE
Hold Left Option (change it in menu > Push-to-Talk Key) and speak. The text
is typed at the cursor as you talk; release the key when you are done.
A waveform shows at the bottom of the screen while you speak.
Language: menu > Language (Automatic, Thai, English).
Punctuation: automatic, or say e.g. "comma", "new line" / Thai "ย่อหน้าใหม่",
"ไม้ยมก" (menu > Punctuation > Show Voice Commands).
MacPTTT starts automatically at login (menu > Launch at Login).

OFFLINE
MacPTTT only uses on-device speech models and works without internet.
If the menu shows "No on-device model", add Thai/English in
System Settings > Keyboard > Dictation so macOS downloads the model.
